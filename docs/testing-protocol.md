# Comprehension testing protocol

A lightweight, moderated protocol for measuring whether visitors understand the
causal story of the guided descent — not just whether they interact.

## Guiding principle

The Finetuned Universe walks a single causal chain across seven thresholds:
low-entropy start → matter survival → stellar nucleosynthesis → galaxy stability →
planetary habitability → abiogenesis → complex life. Comprehension means a visitor
can reconstruct that chain *in order* and distinguish measurement from inference.

## The five tasks

1. **Name the chain.** "As you moved through the chapters, what changed at each
   step to make the next step possible?" Score 1 point per correct causal link
   (max 7). Accept paraphrases that preserve direction.

2. **State the core condition.** "What had to be 'just right' for the story to get
   past the first chapter?" Accept any answer that identifies low initial entropy
   (order) without demanding the word "entropy". Score 0/1.

3. **Distinguish what we know.** "Which of these would a scientist say is measured
   versus modeled?" Present the chapter evidence labels (Observation + model,
   Model-dependent inference, Simplified teaching model, Open scientific question).
   Score 1 point per correctly classified claim (max 3).

4. **Apply one knob.** Reopen a single chapter slider and ask: "If you move this
   outside the band, what breaks and why?" Score 0/1 for a causal explanation
   (not just "it turns red").

5. **Identify what is unknown.** "What is one thing the descent said physics still
   does not explain?" Accept any of the open questions named in the completion
   blocks. Score 0/1.

Total: 0–13. Treat ≥ 9 as "comprehended the causal story"; 5–8 as "partial";
< 5 as "companion but not yet causal".

## Running a session

- Recruit 5 participants, mixed familiarity with physics.
- Neutral opening: "Explore at your own pace. Think aloud if you can."
- Do not correct or prompt until the five tasks.
- Record screen + audio with consent, then transcribe.
- Time-box each task to 2 minutes.

## Observation rubric

| Dimension | What to look for |
|-----------|------------------|
| Causal order | Are chapter boundaries recalled in the correct sequence? |
| Band intuition | Do participants connect slider position to an outcome, not just colour? |
| Uncertainty | Do completion blocks shift "must be X" language to "modeled" language? |
| Abandonment | Where do sessions stall or skip? |
| Vocabulary friction | Which terms (entropy, nucleation, baryogenesis) need a gloss? |

## Reporting

Summarize per task and per chapter, quoted evidence included, and file alongside
the analytics event counts. Keep raw PII out; refer to participants by number.
